## Implemented journeys

- Public listing detail opens a real report form; `/report` also finds/selects real listings. Signed-in client reports appear in their own tracker. Anonymous receipts grant evidence upload/removal only, not case reads. Duplicate, rate-limit, CAPTCHA-ready fail-closed and honeypot paths are handled without publishing flags or counts.
- `/account/complaints`, `/account/complaint-form` and own complaint detail use real APIs. A client can link a published/related property, their own appointment, or a general concern. Transaction subjects and refunds remain unavailable. Contacts, evidence, descriptions and findings are absent from client tracker responses.
- `/staff/complaints` supplies filtering, pagination and refresh; staff case detail has confidential contacts, private evidence grants, assignment, working findings, reviewed evidence, outcomes and public updates. Existing admin case links route into the same role-protected workspace.
- New → triage → investigating → resolved/dismissed. Admin reopen needs a reason and clears working findings to require fresh review. Resolution needs findings, reviewed evidence, outcome and action(s). Permanent rejection/blocking are admin-only and need substantiated/partially substantiated findings. Saving findings stages actions; restrictions apply atomically on resolution. Listing corrections and warning actions record staff work performed separately; this module does not send landlord/client warnings or edit listing descriptions.
- High-risk fake/off-platform-payment reports support an explicit precautionary hold, review due two weekdays later in Africa/Lagos, and explicit release. Counts never trigger holds. Release never republishes automatically; closing or reopening a case never silently clears a hold. Releasing a closed case's hold preserves the resolution time and does not queue another outcome notice.

## Evidence and privacy

Up to three JPEG/PNG/PDF files, at most 5 MiB each. File contents are inspected and image metadata stripped using existing validation. Objects stay in private storage; original filenames and storage keys are excluded from response DTOs. Client uploads require their account plus the scoped token and cannot bypass that through the public report endpoint. Closing a case prevents evidence changes; evidence changes increment the case version to invalidate stale staff edits. Staff-only read grants expire after five minutes and local reads recheck role, case/file scope and grant token. Physical removal uses the existing durable deletion queue. No raw IP addresses or descriptions are logged; report identity keys are HMAC hashes.
